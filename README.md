# Visibian: Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & AI Search Resources

Welcome to the official resource hub for understanding **AI Search Visibility** and **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)**. As traditional Search Engine Optimization (SEO) evolves, modern brands must adapt to how AI search engines—such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google Search Generative Experience (SGE)—retrieve, cite, and recommend content.

Learn more and explore the platform at [visibian.com](https://visibian.com).

## 📌 About Visibian

[Visibian](https://visibian.com) is an AI Search Visibility and Generative Engine Optimization platform designed to help brands track, analyze, and optimize their presence across conversational AI engines. By monitoring brand citations, sentiment, and AI-generated recommendation paths, Visibian enables businesses to capture mindshare in the age of answer engines.
