How to Track Brand Mentions in ChatGPT

A step-by-step guide to monitoring when, how, and why ChatGPT recommends your business.

Why it’s important to track mentions in ChatGPT

Millions of potential customers use ChatGPT daily to compare products, find software recommendations and look up solutions. It’s vital to know what ChatGPT is saying about your brand.

Step-by-Step Mention Tracking Framework

Step 1: Identify Key Commercial Prompts

Brainstorm the non-branded prompts buyers use in your niche. Examples include:

  • "What are the best tools for [industry problem]?"
  • "Compare the top 5 software options for [use case]."
  • "What is an alternative to [Competitor Name]?"

Step 2: Monitor Citation Sources

When ChatGPT provides a recommendation with browsing enabled, check the source links provided. Note which blogs, directories, or review sites are cited to build your digital PR strategy around those targets.

Step 3: Consider the Tone of Recommendations

Ensure that the way your brand is being described is correct. Are they accurately describing your brand including pricing, audience and key features?

Step 4: Use Specialised Tools to Track Mentions

Testing different prompts can take a long time and the results can change depending on the AI. Using automated tools helps you to easily track how many times your brand is mentioned and the sentiment.
