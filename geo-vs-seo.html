GEO vs SEO: A Practical Comparison

Understanding the changes between SEO and GEO

The Evolution from Search to Generation

For over 20 years, search marketing has been about optimising content to help search engine algorithms to rank your page high up in the Search Engine Results Pages. Now that Conversational Assistants and LLMs are becoming the main way people access information, Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) has developed as a way to optimise this content to appear within the Generative Models themselves.

Feature Traditional SEO Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)
Primary Goal Rank on Page 1 (Top 10 Links) Be quoted or recommended by AI
User Query Type Short-tail keywords ("best CRM") Conversational prompts ("What CRM should a 10-person startup use?")
Key Metrics Organic traffic, CTR, SERP position Model Voice, brand sentiment, citation frequency
Content Focus Keyword density, metadata, backlink quantity Quoteable facts, citations, schema, consensus
Key Platforms Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Copilot

How to Optimise your strategy for GEO

1. Be Clear and Concise

Ensure you have defined answers. AI engines will extract the best concise summary.

2. Create Consensus

AI engines will look at facts from across the web. Ensure your main selling points, price points and features are consistent across review sites, press releases, and forums.

Tracking Both Systems Simultaneously

Whilst SEO isn’t dead, it is still important to track the keyword performance and how your content is performing on generative engines. SEO is the base of RAG engines.
